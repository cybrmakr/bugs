Before you hunt

Please read this each time you visit. Programs are shown after you accept.

Terms

  • Test only assets listed as in scope for the specific program.
  • Do not access, change or delete data that isn’t yours. Stop as soon as you reach someone else’s data and tell me.
  • No denial of service, spam, physical attacks or social engineering.
  • Do not disclose findings publicly or to third parties without my written consent.
  • This is a small, personal program. Rules, scope and rewards differ from program to program. Nothing is guaranteed, including rewards and response times.
  • You act at your own risk. I accept no liability for anything you do, to the extent permitted by law.

How it works

  • Pick an open program and check its scope and reward.
  • Hunt within the rules above.
  • Send your report through report.olivermaicher.eu with clear steps to reproduce.
  • Wait for a reply. It can take a while.

FAQ

Is this a big, professional program?

No. It’s a small program I run myself. Things differ between projects and programs, nothing is guaranteed, and replies can take a while.

What is in and out of scope?

Each program lists its own in-scope assets. Anything not listed is out of scope. If you are unsure, ask before testing.

What rewards can I expect?

Each program states its reward range. Amounts, payout method and timing are decided case by case and are not guaranteed.

How long until I hear back?

I aim to reply, but I can’t promise a timeframe. Please be patient and don’t disclose before we’ve agreed on it.

Am I protected if I follow the rules?

If you act in good faith and within scope, I won’t pursue action against you. This is not legal advice, and I can’t bind third parties or authorities. I take no liability for what you do.

Where do I send reports?

Use report.olivermaicher.eu.
